**Style:** BuPulse liquid glass, Geist font, Pulse Blue #2F6BFF → Pulse Violet #7B5CFF, soft blobs in the background. Every screen sits in a floating browser or phone frame. A cursor clicks, zooms follow the action, and labels pop in to explain each step.
**Sound:** a confident male English voice (ElevenLabs). Kinyarwanda subtitles burned in. Light Afro-pop / Amapiano background music (ElevenLabs Music), kept low under the voice. Click, notification and payment-chime effects.
**Screens:** existing features are recorded from the real app with a demo account per business. Features that aren't built yet are designed in the same style: Rwanda pricing, MoMo payment, admin approval, custom plan, and the "Coming soon" store builder and ads manager.
**End card (every video):** BuPulse logo · bupulse.com · WhatsApp +250 791 954 277 · "Book a free demo".
**Prices shown:** Starter 15,000 RWF (card $12) · Business 35,000 RWF (card $27) · Custom from 35,000 RWF + the features you choose.

**Scene order:** the social media version follows the scenes below in order and trims each to its key moment. The in-person version shows every click and adds the steps marked *(demo only)*.

## 1. Intro

1. **Hook** (logo pulse animation): "Posting every day takes hours. What if your marketing ran itself?" Quick montage of finished posts for a boutique, a real estate agency and a phone shop.
2. **Landing on bupulse.com** (phone in hand, then browser): the hero with rotating words "for your shop / salon / agency". Scroll through the features.
3. **Pricing, Rwanda version:** prices in RWF, with "Pay with Mobile Money or card" badges. Three cards: Starter, Business, Custom ("Tell us what you need").
4. **Sign up:** email or Google, then business name, country Rwanda, phone *(demo only: the confirmation email arriving)*.
5. **Onboarding in under 5 minutes:** what you sell, who you sell to, brand voice, logo upload. Colours are taken from the logo automatically. *(demo only: each step at full length)*
6. **Choose a plan:** the camera zooms across the three cards. "Let's see each one." End card.

## 2. Starter: Inzu Fashion (boutique)

1. **Who:** owner Aline, new dresses every week, no time to post.
2. **Choose Starter → choose how to pay:** "Mobile Money" or "Card". She picks Mobile Money.
3. **Pay by MoMo:** the screen shows 15,000 RWF, the MoMo Pay code, the reference `BP-7K3Q` and 3 simple steps.
   - Phone animation: Aline dials, enters the amount and confirms with her PIN. A success message appears.
   - Back on BuPulse she taps **"I've paid"** and types the transaction ID. The screen says "Waiting for approval, usually a few minutes".
4. **Your side (phone animation):** your phone buzzes with an SMS ("You have received 15,000 RWF from ALINE U…, Ref BP-7K3Q"), then an email "New MoMo payment to check".
   - You open the BuPulse admin **Payments** list and tap **Approve**.
   - Aline's screen turns green: "Your Starter plan is active".
5. **What Starter does:**
   - New project: "Kitenge summer dress, 25,000 RWF", 3 photos.
   - **Generate:** an Instagram caption with hashtags, a 30-second video script and 3 ad headlines appear, written in Kinyarwanda and English.
   - **Branded design:** the "New arrival" template with her logo and colours.
   - **Gallery:** photos sorted into folders.
   - **Calendar:** drag the post to Friday 6 pm. *(demo only: editing the caption, regenerating, version history)*
   - **Usage meter:** "12 of 60 texts used this month".
6. **Upgrade hint:** "Need more? Business or Custom." End card.

## 3. Business: Urugo Homes (real estate agency)

1. **Who:** agent Eric, 15 houses and apartments, 3 people on the team.
2. **Choose Business → Mobile Money:** 35,000 RWF. The payment, your approval and the plan switching on are shown quickly, since the full flow is in video 2.
3. **What Business adds:**
   - **Team:** invite 2 colleagues with roles (Admin, Member).
   - **Projects:** a listing ("3-bedroom house, Kibagabaga, 120M RWF") gets a caption, a video script, headlines and a "Just listed" design.
   - **More each month:** 200 texts and 100 designs.
   - **Connect channels:** Facebook and Instagram, one Connect button each.
   - **Calendar:** a full week of listings scheduled; one publishes on time and shows "Published" with the post link. *(demo only: per-network previews, character limits)*
4. **Upgrade hint:** "Want the website chat or Autopilot? Ask for a custom plan." End card.

## 4. Custom: Nova Phones Kigali (phone shop)

1. **Who:** owner Patrick sells phones and accessories, gets many questions on WhatsApp, and wants more.
2. **Ask for a custom plan:** a form (what you sell, your goals, features you're interested in). Then pick a time; a Google Meet invite arrives.
3. **The meeting** (motion graphic of a video call): "We listen first, then build your plan."
4. **Your admin side:**
   - Open Nova Phones → "Customise plan".
   - Tick **Autopilot**: Calendar and Branded designs are added with it automatically ("needed by Autopilot").
   - Add **Website chat + WhatsApp leads**, **AI photo designs** (limit set to 300 a month), **Managed by BuPulse**, and **Analytics**.
   - The total adds up live; round it, then Send.
5. **Patrick accepts the offer:** the offer card shows the price, he accepts, pays by MoMo and you approve (quick version).
   - The badge now says **Custom plan** and the new menu items light up.
6. **Features in action:**
   - **Connect Instagram:** Instagram login → Allow → "Connected".
   - **Autopilot:** "Plan next week" → 7 posts appear (new iPhone arrival, accessories offer, a customer tip, a Sunday deal), each with an AI design → **Approve all** → they publish on schedule.
   - **AI photo designs:** a plain photo of a phone becomes a branded offer poster.
   - **Website chat:** on Nova's website a visitor asks "Do you have the Samsung A56? Price?" The bot answers from the shop's info and asks for a name and number. The lead appears in **Leads**, and Patrick's phone gets a WhatsApp alert.
   - **Managed by BuPulse:** "Our team handles your page": a team member's note in the calendar.
   - **Analytics:** reach, best posts, leads this week.
   - **Coming soon** (labelled): an **online store** built from his products, and an **ads manager** launching a campaign for a new phone.
7. End card.

## 5. Paying by card (Urugo Homes)

1. Choose a plan → **Card**. The price shows "$27/month by card (≈ 35,000 RWF + card costs)".
2. The secure card checkout (Paddle) fills in → "Payment successful" → the plan is active straight away, with no waiting for approval.
3. **Automatic renewal:** the calendar jumps a month ahead → "Renewed automatically, receipt sent". Billing page: receipts, change plan, cancel anytime. *(demo only: switching from card to MoMo at the next renewal)*
4. End card.
