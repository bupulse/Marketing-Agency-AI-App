# Training Lab: the owner's guide

## What it is
The Training Lab is where you teach BuPulse's studio team by example. You add reels, images, recordings, actors and posting habits, and say what is good about each one. When the team plans a reel, a picture, a caption or a post time, it looks at your examples first and follows their style. It never copies your words or numbers. Only you can see the Lab. Nothing here is shown to clients.

## The team of 10
Each member does one job and learns from certain examples.

| Member | What it does | Learns from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Researcher | Gathers the business, product, photos and brief | Nothing; it uses the business's own facts |
| Director | Picks the story, shots, camera and viral recipe | Reels, videos, images |
| Script writer | Writes the reel's lines, title and caption | Reels, videos, Kinyarwanda recordings |
| Designer | Thinks up each picture or post | Images and posts |
| Caption writer | Writes captions and hashtags | Images and posts, Kinyarwanda recordings |
| Painter | Paints start frames and images | Images, actors |
| Filmmaker | Films the AI clips | Nothing directly; it follows the script writer's prompt |
| Editor | Cuts, stickers and captions into the video | Reels, videos |
| Checker | Watches the result and sends back what is wrong | Your "never this" notes, Kinyarwanda words |
| Scheduler | Chooses when to post | Posting habits |

## Adding an example: drop, read, check
1. **Drop** a reel, video, photo or voice note into the big box at the top of the Library, or paste a link.
2. **The AI reads it** (a few seconds): it fills in the title, how it was made, what is good, the techniques, the
   business type, the language and its notes (hook, shots, cuts, beat, stickers, music, words). A link cannot be
   watched, so you fill that one in yourself.
3. **Check** the card: fix anything, tap the stars, add "Never copy this" if needed, and press **Save to the Lab**.
   Discard throws the file away.
Your examples then show as a gallery: tap one to see and change everything about it.

## Adding examples well
Say how it was made and what is good, in your own words. Add a sector and a language, so the right team members find it. Short and specific beats long.

- **Reel or video:** upload the file or paste a link. Describe the shots, the cuts, the music and the hook. Name the technique, for example "price sticker first".
- **Image or post:** upload it. Say what makes the layout, the words or the light work.
- **Kinyarwanda recording:** upload the audio and type exactly what is said, spelled the way it should be written. Then fill in two boxes. "Spellings to avoid": one per line, as "wrong -> right". "Words that are right": brand names, or Kinyarwanda words the checker wrongly flags. The team then fixes your spellings in new reels and flags them everywhere else.
- **Actor:** consent comes first. Tick the box, write the person's name and how and when they agreed. Without consent, the team will not use the actor. For now an actor becomes a presenter only on the test site; on
bupulse.com this stays off until the actor's consent covers appearing for any business. If you have a video of the actor, pause on a clear face and use "Portrait from this video" to make a portrait example from it. If the browser cannot read the video, it says so; then upload a photo instead.
- **Posting schedule:** pick the network, the country, the best hours (like "7, 12:30, 19h"; you see how it was read before you save) and the best days. The team only reorders the posting times the business already allows. How many posts a week stays in the business's own settings.

## Ratings and "never this"
- A rating of 4 or 5 stars makes an example count more. Unrated is neutral.
- A rating of 1 or 2 stars turns it into a warning. The team will not copy it.
- "Never this" is a note on what to avoid. The team and the clip checker read it. Write it as one clear sentence.
- Each example has switches for the engines that may use it. Turn one off and the engine ignores the example.
- **Finished reels** (at the bottom of the Library): press "More like this" on a reel you love, or "Never this" with one
  line on why. The reel becomes an example with that rating, and its video is copied privately into the Lab.
- On the Team page, give stars to what a member made. The member's average shows how well it is doing.

## The Team page
Each member has a card with its job, how many examples it learns from, its latest runs, their cost and your ratings. For the script writer, the designer and the caption writer you can choose the brain: Gemini, Claude on the Anthropic key, Claude on your subscription, or free rules. The other members have one fixed brain. If the chosen brain is not available, the member uses the next one.

**Subscription rule:** the Claude subscription is for your own use and works only on your Mac. Before anything goes to bupulse.com, move every member on the subscription to the Anthropic key. The Team page shows which members are on it now.

## The Editor
Open the Editor tab. It starts with an example video; pick "Open a finished reel" to load a real one. Use the timeline to reorder shots (drag, or the Earlier and Later buttons), trim by dragging the right edge or typing the seconds, and use the panel below for transitions, zoom, words, stickers and the colour look. Undo and Redo are there too. "Export MP4" makes the video in your browser; keep the tab open. Nothing is saved unless you press "Save as a Lab example". That adds your edited video to the Library with a list of what you changed and the reason you give. The reel you opened is never changed.

## What costs money
The AI that reads a dropped example costs about a cent each, inside your $2 test budget, and stops by itself when it is used up. You can always write the notes by hand. Editing and exporting happen in your browser. Reels, pictures and posts cost what they did before, and a member on a paid brain costs per use, within the caps already set.

## First day
1. Add your 3 best reels, rate them 5 stars, and say in a sentence what makes each one work.
2. Add one "never this" about something you hate, for example stock music or text over the face.
3. Add one Kinyarwanda recording with two or three spellings to avoid. Then make a reel and read its lines.
