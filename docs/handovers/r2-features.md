# R2 handover: feature registry and entitlements

Built 9 October 2026 on branch `rwanda-launch`, step R2 of `docs/RWANDA_LAUNCH_PLAN.md`. Later steps (custom plans R3, Rwanda plans R1/R6) only need data: a plan is a list of feature keys.

## What was built

- **`lib/features.ts`**, the one registry (pure data, no server imports). Every feature has a key, label, description, prerequisites, optional usage metric, the outside services it runs on, `built` / `coming_soon`, `app` / `service` (service = sold but nothing in the app to gate: Managed by BuPulse, own Composio account) and the dashboard routes it unlocks. Keys: `content`, `designs`, `aiPhotos`, `calendar`, `gallery`, `websiteChat`, `autopilot`, `analytics`, `canva`, `storeImport`, `driveImport`, `webhooks`, `apiAccess`, `ownComposio`, `managed`, `video` (coming soon), `voice` (coming soon). Helpers: `withPrerequisites`, `dependentsOf`, `validateFeatureSet`, `findCycle`, `featureForRoute`, `sortFeatureKeys`.
- **Plans carry features.** `PlanDefinition.featureKeys` (keys) and `PlanDefinition.highlights` (pricing-card bullets, formerly `features`). `PlanView` / `PublicPlan` expose `features: FeatureKey[]` and `highlights: string[]`; the `capabilities` object is gone from the views (the DB column is kept in step, see below). `SEED_PLANS` in `lib/plans.ts` carries `CORE_FEATURES` and `PRO_FEATURES`.
- **`lib/entitlements.ts`.** `companyEntitlements(businessId)` (per-request cached: what the plan includes, what is usable now, and `resolved`: whether the company's plan was found in the catalog at all), `companyFeatures`, `featureStatus` (`ok` / `not_in_plan` / `service_off` / `plan_unknown`), `blockReason`, `hasFeature`, `assertFeature` (throws `FeatureRequiredError`), `featureBlocked` (message or null, for actions that answer in words), `requireFeature` (redirect), `upgradeHref`, `featureErrorResponse` (403 for `not_in_plan`; 503 while the feature's services are all off or the plan cannot be checked). A feature is usable only when the plan has it and at least one of its outside services is switched on (`isServiceEnabled` is reused through `disabledServices()`).
- **`plan_unknown` is not "lost".** A company whose plan key is missing or not in the catalog (which happens while `getPlans` is degraded to the seed plans and the company is on a custom plan) has `resolved: false`: every gate stays closed (`hasFeature` is false, pages show "Please try again in a moment", routes answer 503), but nothing that would be permanent happens. The publisher fails a claimed post only when the plan is resolved and lacks `calendar`; on `plan_unknown` it hands the post back (`releaseUnsent`, attempt returned) to be retried. The daily store sync stamps a store only on `not_in_plan` and leaves it due on `plan_unknown`; Autopilot claims nothing either way and says why in its note; the webhook retry cron skips deliveries and legacy pending leads untouched (not sent, not failed, not deleted) whenever `webhooks` is not `ok`; the public API answers 503 `feature_unavailable` instead of 403 `plan_required`.
- **Gates.** Pages: `featureGate(key)` in `components/app/no-access.tsx` (shows the "Add to your plan" panel in place). Applied to autopilot, analytics, calendar, channels, content, projects (list, new, detail, edit), designs (plus the AI tab and Canva button by `aiPhotos` / `canva`), gallery, website chat, domains (Cloudflare install), stores, connectors/api, connectors/webhooks; the connectors hub shows "Add to your plan" on cards whose feature is missing. The leads page is the exception: without `websiteChat` it stays open read-only when the company has leads (look, export CSV, delete; no status, notes or notification settings) with an "Add website chat" note, and shows "Add to your plan" only when the inbox is empty. Actions and routes: autopilot, widget settings and check, leads (status, notes and notification toggles; `loadLead`, `removeLeads` and the CSV export are not gated: a company's existing leads are its own data), domains, gallery and imports (Drive/Dropbox = `driveImport`, Canva = `canva`), designs (templates = `designs`, AI = `aiPhotos`), designs preview, calendar/publishing (time zone actions excepted), channels (reminder connect, account picker, social connect/callback), projects (create with generation, generate), `/api/generate`, API keys (`apiAccess`; the public API v1 goes through `featureStatus` in `lib/api/auth.ts`, and `apiFeatureDenied` gates writes per resource: `PATCH /leads/:id` needs `websiteChat`, `POST /posts` needs `calendar`; reads stay open with `apiAccess`), webhooks (create, edit, switch on, test, retry and `emitWebhookEvent`; `deleteWebhook` and `toggleWebhook(off)` are not gated, like disconnecting a store or revoking an API key), stores (actions, Shopify connect/callback, Woo manual and one-click, pending confirm), Canva connect/open/return/callback. Crons: Autopilot skips a company without `autopilot` (and draws templates only without `aiPhotos`); the publisher fails a claimed post of a company whose resolved plan lacks `calendar` with the reason; the leads digest, the daily store sync and the webhook retry cron skip companies without `websiteChat` / `storeImport` / `webhooks` (see `plan_unknown` above for what happens while the plan cannot be checked). Public widget: `loadPublicWidget` is `enabled: false` with `inPlan: false`; `/api/widget/config` answers the neutral `reason: "unavailable"` (the plan is the company's business, not the public's: the detailed reason is in the server log and on the owner's Website chat page; widget.js points there in the console), chat and lead endpoints answer 404 `unavailable` as for a switched-off widget: nothing is answered, nothing is collected.
- **Sidebar and UI.** `NAV_ITEMS` carry `feature` (derived from the route); the sidebar hides items the plan lacks and shows a small "Add to your plan" group (built app features not in the plan) linking to `/dashboard/upgrade?feature=<key>`. That page (`components/app/add-to-plan.tsx`) explains the feature, what it brings with it, and offers "Ask for a custom plan" (Billing for now; R5 adds the request form) to Owners, "Ask your company owner" to others. Role permissions still apply on top.
- **Admin plan form.** A feature checklist (`FeatureChecklist` in `components/admin/plan-form.tsx`): ticking adds prerequisites, a needed feature cannot be unticked ("Needed by X"), coming-soon features are disabled with a badge; highlights are a separate textarea. Server side, `mergePlan` runs `validateFeatureSet` and refuses unknown, coming-soon or incomplete lists with the reason. Jarvis `update_plan` takes `features` and `highlights`; `capabilities` still works as an alias.

## How plans store features

Two new JSON columns: `highlights` (default `[]`) and `featureKeys` (nullable, no default). The old `features` (bullets) and `capabilities` (`{ apiAccess, ownComposio }`) columns stay and are written in step on every save (`featureColumns()` in `lib/admin/plans-service.ts`), so an older build keeps working against the migrated table and anything still reading `capabilities` stays right. A row whose `featureKeys` is NULL (written by a build from before the migration, which does not know the column) is read as the core features plus its capabilities (`cleanFeatures` in `lib/plan-catalog.ts`); an array, even an empty one, is the plan's own list. This is why the column has no default: with `DEFAULT '[]'` a plan created by an older build would have had no features at all. Prerequisites are closed on read, so a plan can never be missing what its features need. Details in `docs/PLANS.md`, "Features and entitlements".

## The migration

`prisma/migrations/20261009110000_plan_features/migration.sql`: adds the two columns (`featureKeys` nullable, see above), copies `features` into `highlights`, and backfills `featureKeys` on every existing row so that nothing changes for existing companies: TRIAL and STANDARD get every built feature of the app except `apiAccess`; PRO gets them all plus `ownComposio`; any other plan gets the core list plus whatever its `capabilities` had switched on. The core list in the SQL is checked against `CORE_FEATURES` by `tests/features/registry.test.ts`, which also checks the column is nullable. No table rewrite, no drops; RLS and grants on `PlanDefinition` are unchanged from `20261009095100_plan_builder_grants`.

**Deploy order (hard rule): run the migration before the new code.** `npx prisma migrate deploy` (with `DIRECT_URL`) first, then deploy the build. While the new code runs against an unmigrated table, reading `PlanDefinition` fails and the catalog serves the seed plans: companies on TRIAL, STANDARD and PRO keep their features (the seeds carry the same lists), but any company on a plan that exists only in the database (a custom or Rwanda plan) is `plan_unknown` for the whole window: every gate is closed for it (pages say "try again in a moment", routes answer 503) and its API keys answer 503. Nothing permanent happens (posts are retried, crons skip, nothing is failed or deleted), but the company cannot use the app until the migration has run. Clients are therefore not "unaffected either way"; the window must be avoided. `npx prisma generate` was run; the migration was **not** applied to any database from this worktree.

## How to add a feature

1. Add the key to `FEATURE_KEYS` and its entry to `FEATURES` in `lib/features.ts` (prerequisites, metric, services, status, kind, routes). The registry test checks there is no cycle.
2. Add an icon to `FEATURE_ICONS` in `components/app/nav.ts` (the type makes this mandatory).
3. Gate it: `featureGate("key")` at the top of its pages, `assertFeature` / `featureBlocked` in its actions, `featureErrorResponse` in its routes, and a skip in any cron that acts per company.
4. Put it in plans: tick it in `/admin/plans` (or `update_plan`), and, if every existing plan should have it, add a data migration that appends the key to `featureKeys` and the key to `CORE_FEATURES` / `PRO_FEATURES`.
5. Tests: `tests/features/registry.test.ts` (shape), `tests/features/entitlements.test.ts` (lookup), `tests/features/gated-endpoints.test.ts` (wiring). Tests whose database mock has no plans grant everything with `vi.mock("@/lib/entitlements", () => import("@/tests/stubs/entitlements"))`; `missing` / `serviceOff` on the stub toggle single features.

## Open items

- `ownComposio` and `managed` are `kind: "service"`: nothing in the app is gated by them. When Composio or the Managed delivery tooling is built, give them routes and gates.
- The "Add to your plan" page links to Billing; R5 replaces the button with the custom plan request form. R3 custom plans only need to write `featureKeys` on the company's private plan.
- Features without their own page (`aiPhotos`, `canva`, `driveImport`) appear in the sidebar group so they can be found; if the group grows too long for small plans, cap it and link to a full list on `/dashboard/upgrade`.
- `components/app/add-to-plan.tsx` imports `UNAVAILABLE_MESSAGE` from `lib/admin/switch` (a server-only module); it is a server component, so that is fine, but it must stay one.
- Public widget chat sessions already open when a plan loses website chat get a 404 `unavailable` on their next message, the same as a switched-off widget; the embedded chat shows its generic "cannot reach" text. A dedicated "not available" line in the iframe would be nicer.
- The webhook retry cron leaves legacy pending leads (`webhookPendingAt`) of a company without `webhooks` in the old queue, unclaimed; they are re-read on every run (100 per run, oldest first). If such a company ever has more than a batch of them, clear `webhookPendingAt` for it by hand or add a sweep.
- Webhooks and API keys pages: after a downgrade, a company with existing endpoints/keys sees a read-only list with an "Add <feature> to your plan" note (`components/app/kept-data-note.tsx`; `readOnly` on the managers keeps only switch off, delete and revoke). An empty page still shows "Add to your plan".
- Members' role gates are unchanged; a Member on a plan without `websiteChat` sees the leads item hidden (feature first, then role).
