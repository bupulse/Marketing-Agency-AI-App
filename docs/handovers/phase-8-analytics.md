# Phase 8 handover: BuPulse's own analytics

Owner-only data, read through `lib/analytics/queries.ts` (admin console and Jarvis). Ranges are inclusive UTC days `{from, to}`; money is USD cents.

- **Tracker:** `public/t.js` is loaded by `app/(marketing)/layout.tsx` only. It sends path, referrer, UTM, screen width and a `sessionStorage` id to `POST /api/collect` and sends nothing when GPC/DNT is on or `navigator.webdriver` is true. The route (public in `proxy.ts`) always answers 204. It drops bots, private paths, foreign origins and rate-limited hashes (120/min). Only `SHA-256(daily random salt | UTC day | IP | UA)` is stored (salt in `AnalyticsSalt`, created on the first visit of the day and deleted by the roll-up once the day has ended; no salt = no row), so no IP or UA is kept. Country comes from `cf-ipcountry`/`request.cf` (dev fallback `ZZ`).
- **Events:** `trackEvent(name, {userId, businessId, sessionId, props})` in `lib/analytics/events.ts` is fire-and-forget (waitUntil on Workers). Hooks: signup and trial in `ensureAccount`, onboarding steps and completion, text, image and design generation, post scheduled and published, lead captured, upgrade, cancel and cancel_scheduled (Paddle webhook). The visit session reaches `signup` through the sign-up form (`visit_session` in Supabase user metadata) or `?vs=` on the Google redirect.
- **Roll-up:** `POST /api/cron/analytics-rollup`, daily 02:17 UTC (`ANALYTICS_ROLLUP_CRON` in worker.ts and wrangler.jsonc). It recomputes yesterday and today, back-fills up to 6 missing days per call (up to 120 days; follow `nextCursor`), and deletes page views older than 90 days. Reads only refresh today (at most once a minute) and yesterday if it was never rolled; older gaps are filled by the cron only. `rollupDay` is idempotent.
- **Back-fill:** sign-ups, trial starts, onboarding completion, feature use, AI cost and revenue come from the original tables, so history fills in. MRR, active subscriptions and churn are snapshots and exist only from the first roll-up on. `paid_starts` and `cancellations` come from events and exist only from this release on.
- **Costs:** AI from `AiCall`; email, hosting and the rest from `MonthlyCost` (`lib/analytics/costs.ts`). They are spread evenly over the days of their month at query time, so an edit applies immediately. Profit = net revenue - AI - fixed.
- **Revenue:** Paddle `transaction.completed` and approved `adjustment.*` refunds from `BillingEvent`, USD only, tax excluded. A refund is counted once per adjustment id.
- **Env:** `ANALYTICS_DEV_COUNTRY` only.
- **Tests:** `tests/analytics/*`. `queries.db.test.ts` needs `RUN_DB_TESTS=1`.
