## Formats

### Identity twist reveal ("you've been watching it")

**Why it works:** Opens like a normal organic post the viewer would watch anyway, then reveals in the last third that the post/graphic/caption they just enjoyed was made by the product. The reveal is the payoff, so people watch to the end and replay. Scored 9/10, our best test. Agencies also rate scenario-first openings and a real reaction pause above scripted pitches; the stale pattern is the ring-light testimonial that reads as an ad in 0.5 s.

| Time | We see | Said |
|---|---|---|
| 0s-3s | Actor in a real shop/kitchen, phone selfie framing, holds up phone showing a pretty Instagram post (real screen recording composited) | "Okay, I have to show you this post. It got more comments than anything I've done this year." |
| 3s-12s | Actor scrolls the real BuPulse feed/calendar recording; the post, then two more | "And then my friend asked, who is your designer?" (beat, small laugh) |
| 12s-24s | Cut to real BuPulse screen: calendar with the posts scheduled, logo on the graphics (real capture, no AI screens) | "Nobody. It writes in my brand voice, puts my logo on it, and it's all in one calendar." |
| 24s-36s | Actor back on camera, real reaction face; on-screen text: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn | "Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, one calendar. I just approve it." |
| 36s-42s | Actor to camera, relaxed | "If you want to see it, comment BUPULSE and we'll send you the link." |

- **Look/camera:** Phone selfie, slightly off-centre, handheld drift, window light, no ring light; unretouched skin; real screen recordings as inserts; captions white bold with dark outline; room tone, no music bed over speech.
- **Suits:** digital service/website, app, beauty, food
- **Real vs AI:** Real: every screen, logo, graphic, post, calendar, the reveal facts. AI-ok: the first-frame actor still then animated, room B-roll. Voice: real recorded Kinyarwanda/English, never AI Kinyarwanda.
- **BuPulse version:** Shop owner shows a post that got attention, then reveals BuPulse wrote it in her brand voice and designed it with her logo, scheduled from one calendar to Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn.
- **Ends on:** Comment BUPULSE and we'll send you the link.
- **Sources:** our-tests: 9 test ads, judge scores (owner briefing 2026-11-11); facebook/tiktok/instagram (agency write-up): https://pixis.ai/blog/ugc-in-2026-whats-working-whats-stale-and-how-to-refresh-your-hook-library/; higgsfield: https://higgsfield.ai/blog/how-to-make-ai-ugc-videos; arcads: /Users/macbook1/bupulse-ugc-ads/research/digital-product-ugc.md

### Loop story (end flows back into the first frame)

**Why it works:** The last line completes the first sentence, so the video replays without the viewer noticing, which lifts watch time and replays. Scored 8/10. East African top performers are also 5-6 s loops and calm one-take tours (2.9M views on a 6 s loop in our library).

| Time | We see | Said |
|---|---|---|
| 0s-4s | Actor mid-action in kitchen/shop, already talking, unboxing a stack of printed flyers vs a phone | "...and that's the reason I stopped doing my posts at midnight." |
| 4s-14s | Cut to her old routine: phone notes, blank screen, tired face (real, acted); on-screen: 'Sunday 11 pm' | "Every Sunday I'd sit with a blank page, what do I even post this week." |
| 14s-28s | Real BuPulse Autopilot screen recording: the week planned, one tap approve | "Now Autopilot plans the week and I approve it with one tap." |
| 28s-38s | Actor relaxed, shows phone with Monday's posts scheduled (real capture insert) | "Posts go out to Instagram and Facebook by themselves, in my voice." |
| 38s-43s | Actor turns to the camera; the frame matches the first frame | "Save this for the next time it's midnight, and..." (cuts to 0:00 line)  |

- **Look/camera:** Single location, one handheld take plus two inserts; the last frame matches the first (same crop, same light); no intro card; caption text same position throughout.
- **Suits:** digital service/website, app, food, beauty
- **Real vs AI:** Real: Autopilot screens and the approve tap. AI-ok: first-frame actor and room, the 'old routine' B-roll. Real human voice only.
- **BuPulse version:** Owner stops doing posts at midnight; Autopilot plans the week, one-tap approve; ends mid-sentence that connects to the opening line.
- **Ends on:** Save this for later.
- **Sources:** our-tests: 9 test ads, judge scores (owner briefing 2026-11-11); bupulse-viral: /Users/macbook1/BuPulse/docs/research/viral/tiktok-notes.md; bupulse-viral: http://localhost:3100/viral (400 rows: tiktok 100, instagram 100, facebook 100, youtube 100); bupulse-viral: /Users/macbook1/BuPulse/lib/video-engine/brain/data/formats.json

### Split-screen reveal (reaction on top, real screen below)

**Why it works:** Top half is a person reacting, bottom half is the real screen doing the work. The viewer sees proof and a human reaction at once. Scored 8/10. Arcads' own tooling and our research confirm the standard pattern for digital products: real UI capture plus a separate actor, composited in the edit, because video models draw screens as gibberish.

| Time | We see | Said |
|---|---|---|
| 0s-3s | Split: actor top (jaw-drop, phone-framed), bottom shows a real blank calendar; text 'My week of posts. 30 seconds.' | "Watch this. I'm not touching anything." |
| 3s-15s | Bottom: real BuPulse screen recording: Autopilot generating the week; top: actor reacting naturally | "It's writing the captions... in my brand voice." |
| 15s-28s | Bottom: graphics appear with the logo; top: actor points at screen | "And it designs the graphics with my logo on them." |
| 28s-37s | Bottom: tap Approve; calendar fills Instagram/Facebook/LinkedIn | "One tap. That's the whole week, three platforms." |
| 37s-43s | Full-frame actor | "Want one? Request a quote and we'll set it up." |

- **Look/camera:** 9:16, 50/50 split, thin divider; actor in phone-selfie framing with imperfect crop; real captures at native resolution (letterbox 'fit' on blurred fill); captions in the actor half's lower third.
- **Suits:** digital service/website, app
- **Real vs AI:** Real: the whole bottom half. AI-ok: top actor (first frame then animate). Real recorded voice.
- **BuPulse version:** Actor reacts while the real Autopilot week plans itself and one tap approves it.
- **Ends on:** Request a quote.
- **Sources:** our-tests: 9 test ads, judge scores (owner briefing 2026-11-11); arcads: /Users/macbook1/bupulse-ugc-ads/research/digital-product-ugc.md; arcads: /Users/macbook1/bupulse-ugc-ads/research/code-teardown.md; higgsfield: https://higgsfield.ai/marketing-studio-intro

### 60-second challenge

**Why it works:** A visible clock gives a built-in hook and stakes ('can I do a week of posts in 60 seconds'). Scored 7/10: strong retention but it needs tight editing, and the clock must be honest.

| Time | We see | Said |
|---|---|---|
| 0s-3s | Phone on a table, a real timer 01:00 on screen, actor leans in | "One minute. A full week of posts for my shop. Go." |
| 3s-18s | Screen recording with timer visible: write posts in brand voice | "First, the captions, in my voice." |
| 18s-32s | Screen: graphics with logo generated; timer at 0:30 | "Now the graphics, my logo, my colours." |
| 32s-40s | Screen: schedule to Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn; timer stops | "Scheduled. Time check..." (show true timer) |
| 40s-44s | Actor shows timer, shrugs | "Comment AD and we'll DM you how." |

- **Look/camera:** Visible real timer overlay (do not fake the time; speed-ramp is ok if labeled 'sped up'); handheld phone over-the-shoulder shots; natural light.
- **Suits:** digital service/website, app, food, beauty
- **Real vs AI:** Real: timer and screen work, actual elapsed time disclosed. AI-ok: actor opening frame only.
- **BuPulse version:** Race the clock to plan, design and schedule a week of posts with BuPulse; disclose any speed-up.
- **Ends on:** Comment AD and we'll DM you.
- **Sources:** our-tests: 9 test ads, judge scores (owner briefing 2026-11-11); tiktok/instagram (agency write-up): https://www.fueler.io/blog/ugc-ad-examples-that-convert-like-crazy-breakdown; instagram/tiktok (agency write-up): https://www.socialync.io/blog/ugc-examples-that-worked-2026

### Selfie 'things I wish I knew' (store-owner shows)

**Why it works:** First-person, mistake-based list that feels like advice from a peer, not an ad: scenario/diagnostic hooks and third-person social proof beat 'this changed my life'. Selfie testimonial scored 6-7/10 in our tests, our weakest, so use it for volume, not as the lead creative.

| Time | We see | Said |
|---|---|---|
| 0s-3s | Selfie, walking in her shop, text '3 things I wish I knew about posting' | "Three things I wish somebody told me about posting for my shop." |
| 3s-14s | Actor to camera, text '1. Post at lunch'; cut to real calendar insert | "One: be consistent. Mine was random, now it's one calendar." |
| 14s-26s | Text '2. Same voice every time'; real post-writing screen | "Two: sound like you. It writes in my brand voice." |
| 26s-37s | Text '3. Don't skip leads'; real website chat saving a lead | "Three: the website chat saves leads while I'm busy." |
| 37s-43s | Selfie close | "Save this, and tap the link in bio if you want it." |

- **Look/camera:** Phone selfie handheld, walking, real background noise, ticked-list text top third; no polished lighting.
- **Suits:** digital service/website, app, beauty, food, physical product
- **Real vs AI:** Real: all screens. AI-ok: actor and shop first frame. No fabricated testimonial numbers or reviews.
- **BuPulse version:** Three tips: consistency (one calendar), same voice (brand voice), no lost leads (website chat saves them), Kinyarwanda version as a variant.
- **Ends on:** Save this, tap the link in bio.
- **Sources:** our-tests: 9 test ads, judge scores (owner briefing 2026-11-11); facebook/tiktok/instagram (agency write-up): https://pixis.ai/blog/ugc-in-2026-whats-working-whats-stale-and-how-to-refresh-your-hook-library/; higgsfield: https://higgsfield.ai/blog/how-to-make-ai-ugc-videos; bupulse-viral: http://localhost:3100/viral (400 rows: tiktok 100, instagram 100, facebook 100, youtube 100)

## Soft CTAs

| Line | Where common |
|---|---|
| Click the link in bio | Instagram and TikTok organic; most common in our East Africa library ('Visit Instagram profile', 'Send WhatsApp message' were top) |
| Comment AD and we'll DM you | Instagram/Facebook keyword-DM automation; global creators |
| Request a quote | Rwanda B2B, our /quote page |
| Save this for later | Instagram/TikTok educational and list content |
| Send this to someone who posts for a shop | Instagram/TikTok share-driven |
| Follow for the next part | TikTok series; global tutorial videos |
| Message us on WhatsApp | Rwanda/Uganda/Kenya shops; the largest local CTA |
| Tap the link, it takes a minute | Facebook/Reels |
| Tell me in the comments which one you'd try | Facebook/Instagram engagement |
| Try it and tell us what you think | Soft product demo, TikTok |

Rule: say it in the actor's own words at the end, once, no pressure.

## Real-human character guide (Rwandan person on a phone)

### Do
- Cast an ordinary adult with specific age, build, short natural hair or braids, not model-perfect
- Realistic skin texture, visible pores, slight shine, a few small blemishes, uneven tone
- Exact wardrobe: 'faded green t-shirt with a small stain, plain cap, simple earrings'
- Real Kigali settings: tin-roofed shop, red dust, motorbikes, plastic chairs, wall posters, mixed clutter
- Soft natural window daylight, mixed light, slightly warm, slightly overexposed highlights
- 'iPhone front camera selfie, one-hand, slightly shaky, autofocus micro-pulses, no stabilization' (Higgsfield Sora 2 guide)
- Asymmetric framing, partly cut-off head, off-centre subject, occasional soft focus
- Natural gestures, blinks, glances away, small laughs, mid-sentence start
- Generate first frame as a still, approve it, then animate it
- Keep the face identical by pinning one avatar/reference image across clips
- Prompt for ethnicity and place as facts: 'Rwandan woman in her 30s, Kigali market'
- Record real Kinyarwanda voice with a real person; use captions

### Do not (AI slop tells)
- No 'cinematic', 'professional', '8K', 'perfect skin', 'flawless', 'studio lighting', 'ring light'
- No symmetrical centered face with glossy skin and a blurred perfect bokeh background
- No airbrushed teeth, uniform smile, plastic hair, identical twin faces across clips
- No clean empty rooms and spotless kitchens; no logos/text drawn by the model
- No AI-drawn screens or UI; use real recordings only
- No stock-photo African clothing/props or generic 'Africa' backgrounds; use actual local items
- No 12-s pure talking head with no cuts, no breathing, no pause
- No AI Kinyarwanda voice or lip-sync
- No fake reviews, fake stats or fake before/afters
- Negatives to paste: 'no beauty filter, no smoothing, no watermark, no extra fingers, no warped text, no studio backdrop, no stock look'
