## The one-line pitch

> **BuPulse makes your posts, photos and videos for Instagram, Facebook and TikTok in 2 minutes, in English, French or
> Kinyarwanda, and you pay with MoMo.**

Three things to repeat in every conversation:

1. **It makes the content, not just ideas.** The caption, the branded design, an AI photo and a short **video made from
   your own product photos**, with captions and a voiceover.
2. **It knows your business.** It learns your products, prices, customers and the Rwandan calendar (pay days, Umuganura,
   Christmas, back to school), so posts sound local, not foreign.
3. **It is made for Rwanda.** Prices in RWF, pay with MTN MoMo or Airtel Money, a free demo with a person, and the
   BuPulse team can run it all for you (Managed by BuPulse).

## The 5-minute demo

For in-person visits (phone in hand) or a Google Meet call. The goal of the demo is **one sentence from the owner: "I
want this"**, then the trial starts on the spot. Practise it until it fits in 5 minutes.

**Before you go (2 minutes):** open the business's Instagram or Facebook page. Note 2 products, how often they post and
whether people ask "price?" in the comments. Have your phone charged, BuPulse signed in, and your referral link ready.

| Minute | You do | You say (plain English; add Kinyarwanda if natural) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Greet, sit down, phone on the table | "Muraho! [RW, check] I'm <name> from BuPulse. I saw your Instagram, your <product> photos are good. Can I show you something in 5 minutes? It's about getting more customers from your posts." |
| 0:30 | Ask 2 questions, listen | "How do you make your posts today? Who does it, and how long does it take?" / "When people ask the price in the comments or on WhatsApp, how fast can you answer?" Write down the answers: you will use them at the end. |
| 1:30 | **Take 2 or 3 photos of a real product** in the shop (or use photos from their page) | "Let's do one of yours right now." |
| 2:00 | In BuPulse: the demo company, a product, add the photos, press generate | "BuPulse writes the caption, the hashtags and the design with your colours. In Kinyarwanda too." Show the Kinyarwanda version. |
| 3:00 | Press **Make a video** | "And this is a short video for Reels and TikTok, made from the photos we just took, with captions and a voice. No editing." While it renders: "Every post on Business can have a video like this: up to 120 a month. They are all kept in your videos library." |
| 3:45 | Open the calendar | "You plan the whole week here, and BuPulse posts it for you on the networks you connect. You approve, it publishes." |
| 4:15 | Show the website chat and leads (Business), mention Social replies is coming | "When someone asks a question on your page, the chat answers from what BuPulse knows about your business and saves their phone number for you. Automatic answers to Instagram comments are coming next." Do **not** promise a date for Instagram replies. |
| 4:30 | Price and the question | "Starter is 15,000 francs a month, Business with videos and the chat is 50,000, and you pay with MoMo. Or the BuPulse team does it all for you. **Do you want to do it yourself, or should we do it for you?**" |
| 5:00 | Close | If yes: sign them up on their phone with your link, choose the plan, start the trial in Admin (or book it). If "later": book the follow-up day now and send the video you just made to their WhatsApp. |

**Tips**

- The video made from **their own product** is the strongest moment. Never demo with a stock example if their product
  is in front of you.
- Use their answer from minute 0:30 in the close: "You said posts take you 2 hours a week. This is 10 minutes."
- If they say "it's expensive", go to [Objection answers](#objection-answers), do not drop the price yourself. Only the
  owner gives discounts (Admin -> Offers).
- If they want "someone to do it", that is a Managed client: the most valuable sale. Book a call with the owner the
  same day.
- End every visit with "Murakoze cyane!" [RW, check] (thank you very much).

## WhatsApp

Send to **businesses** whose number is public (their Instagram bio, Google Maps, shop sign). One message, one
follow-up, then stop if there is no answer. Never add people to groups without asking.

**First message, cold (business number from their page)**

> Muraho <business name>! [RW, check] I'm <your name> from BuPulse, a Rwandan app that makes your Instagram and TikTok
> posts and short videos for you, from your own product photos. I made a quick example for <product> (attached). Would
> you like me to show you how in a free 5-minute demo, at your shop or on a call? Murakoze! [RW, check]

Attach: one design or a 15-second video made in BuPulse for **their** product (use a photo from their public page in
the demo company; tell them so).

**First message, warm (someone gave you their number, or they replied to a post)**

> Mwiriwe <first name>! [RW, check] <friend's name> told me you run <business>. I help shops in Kigali get more
> customers from Instagram and WhatsApp with BuPulse: it writes the posts, makes the designs and short videos, and you
> pay with MoMo. Can I pass by on <day> at <time> to show you in 5 minutes?

**WhatsApp Status (post daily, from your own number)**

- Monday: a before/after: "Their post before / their post with BuPulse" (only with the business's OK).
- Wednesday: a 15-second video made live in a shop: "Made in 2 minutes from 3 photos."
- Friday: a tip: "Post at 19:00 on pay day weekend. People shop when the salary arrives." + "Free demo: <your link>".

**WhatsApp groups (brokers, traders, entrepreneurs)**: only in groups you belong to, and only if the group allows
business posts. Give something useful first:

> Tip for everyone selling on Instagram: a short video of the product gets more views than a photo. If you want, I can
> make one free for your product this week, from 3 photos, in 2 minutes. Send me a message. (BuPulse, <your link>)

## TikTok

TikTok works best with **videos, not DMs**. Post one short video a day from the BuPulse account (or your own):

| Video idea (15 to 30 seconds) | Hook (first 2 seconds, on screen and spoken) |
| --- | --- |
| A real shop's product -> BuPulse video in 2 minutes | "I made a TikTok for this shop in 2 minutes." |
| Weak post vs BuPulse post | "Stop posting like this." |
| A post in Kinyarwanda made by AI | "AI ivuga Kinyarwanda? [RW, check]" (Does AI speak Kinyarwanda?) |
| Pay day tip | "When the salary arrives, post this." |
| The owner's day before/after | "2 hours of posting every week... or 10 minutes." |

End every video with: "Free demo in Kigali. Link in bio." Reply to every comment with an offer to make a free example,
then continue in DMs:

> Thanks for your comment! Send me a photo of your product and I'll make a video like this for you, free. Then if you
> like it, we can do a 5-minute demo.

## Rules for everyone who sells

- **Only the business's public number or a number given to you.** No bought lists, no numbers from groups without
  asking, no messages to private people's personal accounts.
- **One message and one follow-up.** If there is no answer, stop. If someone says no, thank them and stop.
- **Never invent** clients, results, prices, discounts or dates (for example when Instagram replies will be ready).
- **Never take money** in cash or to your own MoMo. Clients pay BuPulse in the app (Billing), with the reference shown.
- **Photos of a business** in a demo are fine; photos of people need their OK.
- **Your link on everything**, so the sale counts for you.
