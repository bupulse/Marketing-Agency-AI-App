# Daily posts made for you

Every night BuPulse makes the day's posts for you, without anyone pressing "Create today's content". In the morning
you open BuPulse and find them under **Posts**, in **Today: needs review**, ready to approve (or already scheduled, for
apps that need no approval).

**Where:** Posts (Today: needs review), Posts → Settings → **Daily posts made for you**. **Plans:** the Business plan
and custom plans, and a free trial of one of them (within the trial's limits). Not while a company waits for its demo
or an invoice, after the trial ended unpaid, or when the account is suspended or being deleted.

## What you see in the morning

- **Posts → Today: needs review**: the day's posts, each on its own app, each with its own idea, caption, hashtags,
  picture or video. The card says **Made for you this morning**.
- **The bell** shows "Your posts for today are ready" while some still wait.
- **An email** (and a phone alert, if alerts are on for your device) from 07:00: "Your posts for today are ready:
  approve them", with a link. It comes once a day, and only when posts wait. No posts made, no email.
- Apps that are not connected yet get a reminder post to post it yourself, as usual. Contest ideas still wait for you
  to confirm the prize and rules.

Approve, edit or reject them as any post. Nothing is posted until you approve, unless you chose in Posts → Settings
that an app needs no approval.

## What they are about

Each day takes the next subject in turn: your running campaigns, your products, then your business in general, so the
days are varied. The idea changes every day (tips, a story, a question, behind the scenes...), never the same as
yesterday's, and the writing uses your Business info, your prices (shown at most about one post in four), your photos
first, and your brand voice, exactly like **Create today's content → AI makes it for me**.

## Your choices (Posts → Settings)

Owners and Admins can change them; Members see them. Changes apply from the next day's posts.

| Choice | Default |
|---|---|
| Daily posts on or off | On |
| Posts per app each day | 1 (up to what your plan allows) |
| Apps | Your connected apps; Instagram and Facebook while none is connected |
| Design style | Free designs (your photos and BuPulse designs); AI photos with people only while your plan's AI images last |

Posts you or Autopilot already planned for the day count: an app that has its post for today gets no more.

## Your allowances are safe

Daily posts use your plan's AI texts (one per post), AI images and videos, but never all of them: what is left this
month is spread over the days left, and daily posts take at most four fifths of it, so you keep some for what you make
yourself and the month never runs out in the first week. A day may be smaller, or skipped, when the month's share is
used; free designs are used when AI images run low.

## For the owner of BuPulse

**Admin → Settings → Daily posts for clients** stops them for everyone at once (no AI is used while off) and says how
many companies got posts today and how many were skipped and why. The same line is on Admin → Content.
