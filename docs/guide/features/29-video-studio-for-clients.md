# The Video Studio, for clients

The Video Studio (**studio.bupulse.com**) makes short videos of your products for Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook and
YouTube Shorts. It reads what BuPulse already knows about your business: your products, prices, photos, phone number
and brand colours. Every finished video lands in your BuPulse **Videos** library, ready to post or schedule.

**Who can use it:** plans with video credits (Trial, Starter, Standard, Business, Pro, and custom plans that include
them). Owners, admins and members can make videos. Other plans see the button with **Upgrade to use**.

## Opening the studio

1. In BuPulse, press **Open Video Studio ↗**. It is in the menu (group **Create**, as **Video Studio**), under **+ Create → New video**, and on the **Videos** page.
2. The studio opens in a new tab, already signed in to your business. There is no second password.
3. After 12 hours the studio asks BuPulse again; press the button once more if it does.

## Making a video

1. **Create**: pick the product. Its photos and price are filled in from BuPulse.
2. Choose where it will be posted (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or YouTube Shorts) and the shape: 9:16 (vertical) for
   Reels, TikTok and Shorts, 1:1 or 4:5 for Facebook and Instagram feeds.
3. Optional:
   - **Phone clips**: add 2 to 4 short clips from your phone (5 to 10 seconds each): your shop, hands holding the
     product, you talking about it. Free.
   - **AI makes the clips**: Google Veo makes 2 or 3 real moving clips from your product photo (see the prices below).
   - **Voice-over** (English, French and other major languages; the voice cannot speak Kinyarwanda or Swahili yet,
     so for those the words on screen and the caption stay in your language and the voice speaks English),
     and **music** from BuPulse's library.
   - **Your own line**: what the video should say or do ("say we deliver in Kigali").
4. Press **Generate**. The button shows the price in credits before you press it.
5. Open the design to see **Why this video**: which viral techniques it uses. Change anything you like, then press
   **Make MP4** (free).

Every video is designed from the techniques of real viral videos (mostly from Rwanda and East Africa): how they open,
how fast they change, where the price and number go. Two videos of the same product come out different. Prefer a fixed
look? Press **Use a template instead**. More in [Viral recipes](22-viral-recipes.md).

## What it costs

1 credit = 10 RWF. Your plan includes credits every month (Trial 50, Starter 100, Standard 100, Business 500, Pro
1,500); unused credits do not roll over. Buy more in BuPulse, **Billing** (MoMo top-up). The studio's **Buy credits**
link goes there.

| Action | Credits |
|---|---|
| Design one Motion video | 35 |
| Voice-over (one take; the same lines again are free) | 15 |
| AI image or AI presenter shot | 15 |
| AI clip (Google Veo), 4 / 6 / 8 seconds | 65 / 95 / 130 |
| Phone clips, music, changing the shape, making the MP4 | free |
| Premium 3D clip | "coming soon" |
| Seedance AI video | "coming soon" |

Examples: a Motion video with a voice-over is 50 credits. With three 4-second AI clips it is 245 credits. A step that
fails (Google cannot make a clip, for example) gives its credits back.

## Where your videos go

- The MP4 is saved in your BuPulse **Photos**, folder **Video Studio**, and appears in **Videos** with its caption
  and hashtags.
- BuPulse rings the **bell** and sends one email ("Your video is ready") for videos you have not opened yet.
- From **Videos**: play, download, **Schedule** to your connected pages, or delete (not while a scheduled post still
  uses it). Autopilot can post library videos too.
- Your phone clips are in the Gallery folder **Studio clips**, AI clips in **AI clips**, AI images in **Studio images**.
  They count toward your Gallery space.

## Good to know

- All facts (price, number, place) come from your product and business details. Check them in BuPulse before you make
  a video; the studio warns about any number it could not find there.
- Nothing is posted until you schedule it.
- The studio never shows other people's videos or account names; it only uses what made them work.

## If something goes wrong

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Upgrade to use" | Your plan has no video credits. Choose a plan or add video credits in **Billing**. |
| "Ask your company owner" | Your role cannot make content. The owner or an admin can change it in **Team**. |
| Not enough credits | Buy credits in **Billing**, or turn off AI clips to make the video cheaper. |
| "AI clips are paused right now" | The video is made from your photos and your own clips; AI clips come back later. |
| A clip is refused | MP4 or MOV, 5 to 10 seconds, at most 30 MB. On iPhone: Settings, Camera, Formats, **Most Compatible**. |
| The studio asks you to sign in | Go back to BuPulse and press **Open Video Studio ↗** again. |
