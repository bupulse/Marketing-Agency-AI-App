# Videos and voiceover

BuPulse makes short vertical videos (10 to 30 seconds, 1080x1920) for posts: a slideshow of the business's photos and
the AI people photo, with moving captions, a spoken voiceover in the chosen voice, and background music. The owner does
not need any video skills: on a post's "Pictures and videos" panel BuPulse writes the script if there is none, plans the
scenes and draws the video, either in the owner's own browser or automatically by the **video robot**. Real AI video
(clips generated by an AI video model) is **coming soon**: the button is shown but switched off.

**Page:** no page of its own. Videos are made on the Content day view (`/dashboard/content`, "Pictures and videos"),
on Autopilot cards (`/dashboard/autopilot`, "Make the video"), and the company voice is chosen in Content → Settings
(`/dashboard/content/settings`).  **Plans:** Business (feature keys `video` and `voice`): 120 videos a month, at most
4 a day, each up to 30 seconds, with a voiceover on each. Not in Starter: it can be added in Billing → Add features
(Rwanda seed prices: Videos 20,000 RWF a month, Voiceover 15,000 RWF a month, Admin → Add-on prices) or in a custom
plan. The free trial has one 30-second sample video. The admin can change all of this in Admin → Plans.

## What you see

![A product's content day with its pictures and videos](../images/client/content-products-detail.jpg)

The video panel (under a post's picture on the Content page, or **Picture and video** on an Autopilot card) shows:

- the video itself, or a progress bar while it is drawn;
- badges such as **Video ready to render**, **Being made automatically** ("usually within 30 min") and **Video ready**;
- **Make it now** (make it in this browser instead of waiting for the robot), **Make the video**, **Make the video with
  my script** (your own lines, one per scene);
- **Edit captions**, **Make it again**;
- the voice: on or off, which voice, **Regenerate voiceover**, and this month's voiceover characters;
- the music: the chosen track, **Add your music** or another track, or no music.

Without video in the plan the panel shows the picture and **Add video to your plan**.

## How to use it

1. Choose your company voice once in **Content → Settings** (press Listen to hear each voice).
2. Open a product or campaign on the **Content** page. For a video idea, the panel starts making the video by itself
   (or the robot makes it within about 30 minutes).
3. For any other post, press **Make a video**. BuPulse writes a short script from the post (1 AI text credit) and
   makes the video. Or write your own script: one line per scene.
4. Watch it. Change the captions, the music or the voice and press **Make it again** if you want.
5. Schedule the post as usual. On Instagram it goes out as a Reel and on Facebook as a Page video. TikTok and YouTube
   get a reminder email with a download link for the video (7 days).

## Limits and settings

| What | Counts against |
|---|---|
| A video that is made and saved | 1 of the month's **videos** (120 on Business) and 1 of the day's (4) |
| Making the same video again (captions, photos, music, voice) | nothing extra, it replaces the old one |
| A script written by BuPulse | 1 AI **text** credit (given back if it fails) |
| The spoken voiceover | **voiceover characters** (600 per video; Business 72,000 a month); speaking the same text again is free |
| Regenerate voiceover | a new take: at most 3 a day per video, 30 seconds apart |

- Each video is at most 30 seconds and 8 MB.
- Published videos are removed from BuPulse **30 days after posting** (they stay on the social accounts). Videos count
  toward the media storage limit until then (500 items, 2 GB).
- Without the ElevenLabs key, or when the admin switches ElevenLabs off, videos are made with captions and music only.
- If the music library is empty, videos have no music.
- What the admin can change: videos a month and per day (Admin → Plans), add-on prices (Admin → Add-on prices), the
  ElevenLabs switch and keys (Admin → Services), the music library (Admin → Music), and the video robot status
  (Admin → Services, "Video robot").

### The sales room screen (`/screen/<link>`)

`/screen/[token]` is not a client page: it is the BuPulse sales team's TV (live payments, trials to follow up, the
leaderboard) opened with a secret link made in **Admin → Organisation → Sales screen**. It is listed here because its
celebration sounds can be made with ElevenLabs sound effects (`generateSoundEffect`, lib/screen/sounds.ts). An unknown
or revoked link answers 404.

### Key functions

- `videoAvailable` (lib/visuals/service.ts) — the plan has `video` and more than 0 videos a month.
- `remakeContentVisuals` (lib/visuals/content.ts) — "Make a video": writes a script when there is none (`writeContentScript`, lib/visuals/script-writer.ts), then plans the video.
- `buildVideoPlan` (lib/visuals/video-plan.ts) — script → scenes, durations (10 to 30 s), photos, captions, brand colours.
- `startVideoRender` (app/dashboard/visuals/actions.ts) and `leaseVideoForBrowser` (lib/visuals/service.ts) — the browser takes the render lease and gets photo and audio URLs.
- `ensureVoiceover` (lib/voice/service.ts) — speaks the captions once with ElevenLabs (`getTtsProvider`, lib/ai/tts.ts), stores a `VoiceClip`, bills `voiceCharacters`.
- `fitPlanToVoice` and `musicEnvelope` (lib/voice/speech.ts), `mixSamples` (lib/voice/mix.ts) — scenes in time with the words; music ducked under the voice.
- `trackForVideo` (lib/music/service.ts) and `pickTrack` (lib/music/tracks.ts) — a library track for the post's mood.
- `prepareVideoUpload` (lib/visuals/service.ts) — checks plan, videos left (month and day), quota and size before any upload.
- `finalizeVideo` (lib/visuals/service.ts) — reads the real length, counts the video once (`consumeUsage(…, "videos")`, `consumeDailyVideo`), puts it on the posts, runs `attachToContent`.
- `videosLeftNow` (lib/plans.ts) — the lower of this month's and today's videos left.
- `claimRobotVideos`, `completeRobotVideo`, `failRobotVideo`, `robotStatus` (lib/visuals/robot.ts) — the video robot's lease, completion, failures (3 at most) and admin status.
- `robotGuard` (lib/visuals/robot-auth.ts) — constant-time bearer check; the robot API is 404 without the secret.
- `purgePublishedVideos` and `purgeAbandonedUploads` (lib/visuals/cleanup.ts) — the 30-day clean-up and abandoned uploads.
- `getAiVideoProvider` (lib/visuals/ai-video.ts) — returns null: AI video is coming soon (`AI_VIDEO_STATUS`).
- `screenByToken` (lib/screen/links.ts) — the sales TV link check.

### How it works

1. The panel (`content-visual.tsx`) calls `startVideoRender` → lease → `ensureVoiceover` (ElevenLabs, if `voice` is in
   the plan and characters are left) → the plan, signed photo URLs and audio URLs.
2. `video-renderer.ts` draws the scenes on a canvas at 30 fps, mixes voice and music, and encodes MP4 (or WebM).
3. `startVideoUpload` → `prepareVideoUpload` (reservation, signed upload URL in the `media` bucket) → the browser
   uploads → `finishVideoUpload` → `finalizeVideo` (checks the file, counts usage, attaches to posts).
4. Video robot instead: GitHub Actions runs `robot.mjs` → `POST /api/robot/videos/claim` → headless Chrome draws with
   the same renderer → uploads → `POST /api/robot/videos/complete` → `finalizeVideo`. "Make it now" takes over unless
   the robot claimed it less than 5 minutes ago.
5. When the post is published, Instagram gets a Reel and Facebook a Page video
   ([Calendar and publishing](07-calendar-and-publishing.md)); 30 days later `purgePublishedVideos` deletes the file.

## Claude-designed videos (in testing)

A new engine is being tested outside the app, in the **Video Lab** (`npm run lab`, http://localhost:3100, TEST
database only). Claude looks at the product and its photos and designs the whole video: scenes, moving photos,
animated text, shapes, colours, transitions, music mood, caption and hashtags. BuPulse draws it and makes the MP4 for
free. One design costs about $0.05 to $0.12. Clients do not see it yet; it reaches the "Pictures and videos" panel once
the tests are done. How it works and where the code lives: [docs/VIDEO_ENGINE.md](../../VIDEO_ENGINE.md).

## If something goes wrong

| Symptom | First place to look | Usual fix |
|---|---|---|
| "Add video to your plan" on the panel | Billing → Add features; `videoAvailable` | Add the Videos add-on or move to Business |
| "4 videos today, the most a day" | Usage page; `prepareVideoUpload`, `videosLeftNow` | Wait until tomorrow (Kigali day) or raise "Videos per day" in Admin → Plans |
| Video has no voice | Admin → Services (ElevenLabs switch and key); the panel's note; `ensureVoiceover` | Switch ElevenLabs on, check characters left, or add the Voiceover add-on |
| Video has no music | Admin → Music (library empty or tracks inactive); `trackForVideo` | Upload or activate tracks |
| "Being made automatically" for hours | Admin → Services, "Video robot" (last run, failures); `.github/workflows/video-robot.yml` | Press Make it now; check `VIDEO_ROBOT_SECRET` and `ROBOT_APP_URL`; re-enable the workflow |
| "We could not make it automatically" | `ContentVisual.robotAttempts` (3 failures); `failRobotVideo` | Make it in the browser with Make it now |
| WebM video without sound | The browser has no WebCodecs (old browser) | Use a recent Chrome, Edge or Safari |
| Old video gone from BuPulse | `purgePublishedVideos` (30 days after posting) | Normal; it is still on the social account |
| Sales TV link shows 404 | Admin → Organisation → Sales screen; `screenByToken` | Make a new link (old one revoked) |
