# The support team: who answers BuPulse's help chat

BuPulse has its own help chat on every page (except the admin console). The AI answers first, day and night. When it
cannot help, or someone asks for a person, it hands the chat to **the support team**: people who answer in the
**support app** at **support.bupulse.com**.

**Where:** you set the team up in **Admin → Live chat**. The team works in the support app (support.bupulse.com; until
that address is set up, at **bupulse.com/support**).

Support agents have **their own accounts**. They never use the admin console, and an admin or staff address cannot be
an agent: give each agent a separate work address.

## Add an agent

1. Open **Admin → Live chat → Agents**.
2. Under **Add an agent**, fill in the name and the **work email**.
3. Choose the **role**:
   - **Agent**: answers chats in their departments.
   - **Supervisor**: sees every chat, moves chats between people, reads chats live, and sees the stats.
4. Tick the **departments** (Sales, Technical help, Billing, Creators). They decide which chats reach the agent. A
   supervisor needs none.
5. Press **Send invite**.

The agent gets an email. The link opens the support app, where they:

1. press **Choose my password** and choose a password (at least 10 characters, with a letter and a number);
2. add an **authenticator app** on their phone (Microsoft Authenticator, Google Authenticator or 1Password) by scanning
   a QR code.

From then on, signing in is the **work email + password**, then the **6-digit code** from the app. The agents list shows
**6-digit code: On** once it is set up.

If the email did not arrive (or the link expired), press **Resend invite** on their row: the old link stops working.

## Change, lock or reset an agent

On the agent's row:

- **Edit**: name, role, departments, and how many chats they take at once (1 to 20).
- **Lock**: they are **signed out at once** and cannot sign in until you press **Unlock**. Use it when someone leaves,
  or loses a laptop.
- **Reset authenticator**: for a lost or new phone. Their authenticator is removed and they are signed out. At their next
  sign-in they add the app again (after a code sent to their email).

An agent who forgot their password uses **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page; they still need their 6-digit code.

Every change is in **Admin → Audit log**.

## Departments, team hours, greetings and the AI

- **Departments:** rename them if you like. The four departments always exist, because the AI sorts chats into them.
- **Team hours** (Kigali time): the days and hours a person answers. The chat shows them ("team open until 20:00").
  Outside them the AI keeps answering; when it hands over, it says when the team opens and that the reply comes by
  email or WhatsApp. The stats count reply times inside team hours only.
- **Greetings:** the little "Need help?" line that appears near the chat button. One per page: the page path (for
  example **/pricing**), the text, after how many seconds it shows, and up to 3 quick replies. The page **\*** is for
  every other page.
- **AI rules:** turn the AI on or off, write how it should talk (tone and rules, apart from the guide), list the topics it
  always hands to a person (for example refunds or payment confirmation), and the support **WhatsApp number** for
  "Continue on WhatsApp" (with the country code, like +250788000000; empty hides the button).

## Working in the support app

- **Online / Away / Offline** at the top: only online agents get new chats.
- **Chats:** **Mine** (yours), **Waiting** (handed over, nobody has answered), **AI** (the AI is answering), **All**.
  Upset people and hot leads go to the top, with tags for the department, the language and how long they have waited.
  In a chat: the **AI summary**, **3 suggested replies**, **Translate**, **Friendlier**, **Shorter**, and an **Internal
  note** tab (orange, the client never sees it). **Give back to AI**, **Transfer** (to a colleague or a department, with
  a note) and **Close chat** are at the top. On the right: their account, this visit (where, device, pages) and past
  chats.
- **Live visitors:** who is on BuPulse now, what page they are on, and how they came. **Start chat** writes to someone
  who is browsing; **Join chat** opens a chat that is going on.
- **Stats** (supervisors): chats, how many the AI solved alone, hand-overs, first reply time, ratings, new leads, and
  who on the team is online. **Knowledge gaps** (questions the AI could not answer) is coming soon.

## Install the support app and get alerts

The support app installs like an app: its own icon, full screen, nothing from a store.

- **Computer (Chrome or Edge):** open the support app and press **Install the app** in the menu on the left (or the
  install icon in the address bar).
- **Android (Chrome):** menu **⋮** → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
- **iPhone (Safari):** **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**, then open **BuPulse Support** from the Home
  Screen. Apple only sends alerts to apps on the Home Screen, so do this **first**.

Then press **Get alerts on this device** (bottom left; on a phone under your name at the bottom) and allow
notifications. A new chat or a hand-over plays a sound and shows an alert while the support app is open (also in a
background tab), and a phone alert when it is closed. Nothing from the chats is stored on the device.

## Good to know

- The support app is never shown to search engines.
- Locations of visitors are approximate (from the internet address). IP addresses are kept 30 days, then only the
  country. The privacy policy explains this.
