Guide

How to make videos in the studio

1. Two engines

Seedance 2.0 (real video) makes real-looking footage: people move, hands hold the product, the camera travels through a place, and the clip has sound. It takes 1 to 10 minutes and costs the most credits. Motion (Claude) designs an animated video from your photos in about a minute: camera moves, text, shapes, music and captions. It is instant and cheap. Use Seedance when you need people and action; use Motion for offers, lists, product reveals and everything you post every week.

2. Making a Seedance video

  1. Mode. Text (describe everything), Image (a photo is the first frame), Reference (photos, videos and audio the model should follow), First + last frame, Edit (change a video you made) or Extend (continue it).
  2. References. Pick product photos, or upload photos, videos and audio. Refer to them in the prompt as Image 1, Video 1, Audio 1, in the order shown. No real faces: Seedance refuses them. Presenters come from the character library.
  3. Prompt. Say what happens, in what order, with what camera and sound. Press Write it with Claude to get a shot list from your product's facts.
  4. Quality. Mini (cheapest), Fast, Pro (best, up to 1080p). Duration 4 to 15 s. Resolution and aspect ratio. Sound on or off. Fixed lens keeps the camera still. Offline is slower and cheaper. Draft makes a quick 480p preview you can then upgrade.
  5. Generate. The credits are taken; a failed video gives them back. You can leave the page.

3. Writing good Seedance prompts

  • Structure by time: "0–2 s: …; 2–5 s: …".
  • Name the subject, the action, the place, the light, the camera move and the sound.
  • Reference assets by number: "the woman in Image 1 holds the phone from Image 2".
  • Keep the product exactly as in its photo: "same shape, colours and label as Image 2".
  • Ask for dialogue with quotes: Character speaks: "…".

4. Making a Motion video

Open Motion. Pick a product, up to 4 photos, a template, a hook, the shape and the length. Add a cartoon presenter (free) or a realistic AI presenter shot. Press Generate; play it; edit or extend it by describing the change; make the MP4 at 480p, 720p or 1080p.

5. Credits

1 credit = 10 RWF. See Pricing for the rates. The lab wallet is pretend money for testing.

6. FAQ

Why does Seedance refuse my photo?

It contains a real person's face, or something its content rules do not allow. Use the product on its own, or a presenter from the character library.

Why are motion videos so much cheaper?

Claude only writes the design; BuPulse draws the video itself. Seedance runs a large video model on GPUs for every second of footage.

Can I use a Seedance video as a reference?

Yes: Edit and Extend use the finished video as Video 1. Any video you made can be a reference for a new one.

Where do finished videos go?

They are saved in the studio (My videos) and will go to the BuPulse gallery and scheduler when the studio joins the app.
