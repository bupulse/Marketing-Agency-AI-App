How to make videos in the studio

1. Three ways to make content

Seedance 2.0 (real video) makes real-looking footage: people move, hands hold the product, the camera travels through a place, and the clip has sound. It takes 1 to 10 minutes and costs the most credits. Claude 3D motion designs an animated video from your photos in about a minute: camera moves, 3D text, shapes, music and captions. It is instant and cheap. Nano Banana makes a realistic image from a prompt and up to 3 photos. Use Seedance when you need people and action; use Motion for offers, lists, product reveals and everything you post every week; use images for posts and as the first frame of a video.

2. Making a Seedance video

  1. Mode. Text (describe everything), Image (a photo is the first frame), Reference (photos, videos and audio the model should follow), First + last frame, Edit (change a video you made) or Extend (continue it).
  2. References. Pick product photos, or upload photos, videos and audio. Refer to them in the prompt as Image 1, Video 1, Audio 1, in the order shown. No real faces: Seedance refuses them. Presenters are fictional.
  3. Prompt. Say what happens, in what order, with what camera and sound. Press Write with Claude to get a shot list from your product's facts.
  4. Quality. Mini (cheapest), Fast, Pro (best, up to 1080p). Duration 4 to 15 s. Resolution and aspect ratio. Sound on or off. Fixed lens keeps the camera still. Offline is slower and cheaper. Draft makes a quick 480p preview you can then upgrade.
  5. Generate. The credits are taken; a failed video gives them back. You can leave the page. Until the BytePlus key is added, Generate shows what the clip will be and offers a Motion video instead.

3. Writing good Seedance prompts

  • Structure by time: "0–2 s: …; 2–5 s: …".
  • Name the subject, the action, the place, the light, the camera move and the sound.
  • Reference assets by number: "the woman in Image 1 holds the phone from Image 2".
  • Keep the product exactly as in its photo: "same shape, colours and label as Image 2".
  • Ask for dialogue with quotes: Character speaks: "…".

4. Making a 3D motion video

Open Claude 3D motion. Pick a product, up to 4 photos, a template, a hook, the shape and the length. Add a cartoon presenter (free) or a realistic AI presenter shot. Press Generate; play it; edit or extend it by describing the change; make the MP4 at 480p, 720p or 1080p.

5. Making an AI image

Open Nano Banana. Describe the picture, add up to 3 product photos or uploads, choose the shape and press Generate. The image is saved in Your images; press "Use in a video" to send it to the generator as a reference.

7. FAQ

Why does Seedance refuse my photo?

It contains a real person's face, or something its content rules do not allow. Use the product on its own, or a fictional presenter.

Why are motion videos so much cheaper?

Claude only writes the design; BuPulse draws the video itself. Seedance runs a large video model on GPUs for every second of footage.

Can I use a Seedance video as a reference?

Yes: Edit and Extend use the finished video as Video 1. Any video you made can be a reference for a new one.

Where do finished videos go?

They are saved in the studio (My videos) and will go to the BuPulse gallery and scheduler when the studio joins the app.

Can I call the studio from my own software?

Yes, see the API page. Keys per business come when the studio joins the app.
