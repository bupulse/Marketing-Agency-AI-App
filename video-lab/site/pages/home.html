FAQ

Questions

What is Seedance 2.0?

ByteDance's video model. It makes real-looking video from text, photos, videos and audio, with sound. BuPulse uses it through BytePlus, ByteDance's official cloud service.

What is the difference between a real video and a motion video?

A real video is footage made by Seedance: people and things actually move. A motion video is designed by Claude from your photos: camera moves, animated text and graphics, like a professional ad made in an editing app. Motion videos are instant and cost a few credits; real videos take 1 to 10 minutes and cost more.

Can I show my staff or myself in a video?

Not yet. Seedance does not accept photos of real faces. Presenters come from the BytePlus character library. Verified real-person assets may come later.

Which shapes and lengths are supported?

9:16, 3:4, 1:1, 4:3, 16:9 and 21:9; 4 to 15 seconds for a Seedance clip, 4 to 30 seconds for a motion video.

Are the videos watermark-free?

Yes.

How are credits counted?

Each video shows its credits before you generate: by length, quality and engine. A failed generation gives the credits back.
